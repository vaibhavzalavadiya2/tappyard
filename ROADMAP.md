## 🧠 Part 1: First Principles & Second-Level Analysis

### 1. First Principles (Fundamental Human Realities)
* **Zero Friction (< 3s Rule):** Web users do not tolerate loading bars or account walls. First dopamine hit must occur within 3 seconds of landing.
* **Context of Play:** People play on subways, during 5-minute study breaks, or in bed. Games must be one-thumb friendly, work offline, and have instant discreet mute.
* **The Zeigarnik Effect:** Retention comes from *Unfinished Business* (near-misses, high-score revenge), not from having 1,000 mediocre games.
* **Portal Sunk Cost:** Diamonds and accomplishments in Game 1 must carry value across the entire portal.

### 2. Second-Level Thinking (The Competitive Edge)
* **No Clones, Distinct Identity:** Rather than a boring copy of 2048, *2048 Neon Fusion* features musical scale synthesis (merging tiles plays rising harmonic notes) and a "Diamond Undo" powerup powered by TapYard diamonds.
* **Zero-Asset Procedural Performance:** No 15MB MP3s or PNGs. Everything is Web Audio synthesizers and vector CSS/canvas, keeping initial bundles under 350 kB gzip for sub-second loads on mobile.
* **Viral Challenge Loops:** High-score screens generate direct 1v1 challenge links for WhatsApp/Discord (*"Can you beat my 1,480m on TapYard?"*).
* **Zero Intrusive Ads on Day 1:** Preserve pure player trust and word-of-mouth growth.
